## How to Use (New Workflow)

### Step 1: Start
- Open the application
- Select your preferred **spoken language** (English, Hindi, or Marathi)
- Select preferred **text language** for the final image

### Step 2: Record
- Click the mic button to record your description
- Speak naturally about your business:
  - ✅ Give your business name
  - ✅ Describe what you offer
  - ✅ Mention specific items (e.g., "red balloons for birthday parties")
  - ✅ Share your phone number if you want it displayed
  - ✅ Mention any offers or discounts

**Example**: "This is Swadisht Bakery. We make custom cakes and pastries. Our specialty is red velvet cakes with gold decorations. You can reach us at 9876543210. We offer 20% discount on orders above 500 rupees."

### Step 3: Review & Edit ⭐ **NEW**
- AI automatically extracts details from your recording:
  - Business name
  - Tagline
  - Services/Features (up to 3)
  - Contact information
  - Call-to-Action button text
  - Layout style

- **Edit anything** that's not quite right:
  - Fix business name spelling
  - Add/remove/modify services
  - Update contact details
  - Change the tagline
  - Choose layout style (Elegant, Bold, Sidebar, Minimal)

### Step 4: Confirm & Preview
- Click "Confirm & Preview" to generate the image
- After generation, preview your design
- **Download** when satisfied

## Best Practices

### ✅ DO:
- Speak clearly and naturally
- Mention specific items (balloons, jewelry, etc.)
- Include your business name at the beginning
- Say your phone number if you want it displayed
- Mention any special offers or discounts
- Review extracted details before confirming

### ❌ DON'T:
- Whisper or speak too fast
- Background noise will affect transcription
- Don't forget to specify items you want visible
- Don't rush the review step - edit details if needed

## Troubleshooting

### Audio not recording?
- Check microphone permissions in your browser
- Try refreshing the page
- Ensure microphone is working

### Text overlay unclear?
- The system automatically chooses colors for visibility
- If still unclear, try a different layout style
- Check lighting in background image

### Details not extracted correctly?
- Edit them in the Review screen! ✏️
- Try speaking more clearly next time
- Mention specific items explicitly

### Same design repeated?
- Each generation uses a random seed for variation
- Try changing layout, colors, or business details
